# antislop audit 002 - Loadout 0.5.1, the built app

Date: 2026-11-02. Mode: AFTER (audit only; nothing in the app was changed).
Scope: `work/loadout/index.html` (7,126 lines, 0.5.1), `sw.js`, `manifest.json`,
`README.md`, checked against the antislop core (Delivery Gate blocks 1 to 4),
antislop-ui (App and Dashboard), antislop-human, antislop-layoutmobile,
antislop-copywriting, and PLAN.md sections 11 and 16.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate and anything that blocks a task or
loses data = HIGH; Purpose-Gate, cross-view consistency, and readability-rule
misses = MEDIUM; Quality Locks, copy, and polish = LOW. Nothing is changed
until you pick numbers.

How it was checked: the work-site preview server (port 8742), `/loadout/` with
`?demo=1&costs=1`, `&role=tech|accounting|warehouse`, `&board=1`, `&empty=1`,
`&print=1`. The service worker was unregistered and its caches deleted before
the first load. A width script (scrollWidth against innerWidth, every visible
element's right edge, every control's height under 641px) ran over 35 views
at 360, 375, 640, 900, 1280, and the pane width (521), and over 34 modals at
375. Contrast ratios come from `contrast-check.py`; `color-mix()` values were
resolved by hand in sRGB over the surface they sit on. The camera is blocked in
the pane and is not counted as working. The pane reports
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, so animations were verified in the CSS.

## Findings

### HIGH (Hard Gate, blocks a task, or loses data)

1. **R-25 non-text / PLAN 11 "3:1 for control borders": every field and
   button edge fails.** `.fld`, `.btn`, `.chev` borders are `--m-line2`
   #c7d0da: 1.56:1 on white, 1.41:1 on card2. The `.btn` fill (#f1f4f8) on a
   white card is 1.10:1, so a secondary button is found by its text only. Every
   view, every form. Fix: `--m-line2:#808b99` (3.46:1 on white, 3.14:1 on
   card2, both checked) for control borders only, keep `--m-line` for dividers.
2. **R-25 grey status pill fails on tinted surfaces.** `.st` with no state
   class (#5b6675 on muted 14%) is 4.40:1 on card2 and 4.47:1 on a `.need` row.
   Seen on: Tracking "Ordered" pill on a package card (card2), "Draft" in the
   Orders list on the vendor-less draft row (need row), "Open" pill on Tickets
   rows with lines to order (need row), "Installed" and "Retired" in expanded
   panels (card2), the location kind pill in the location panel. Fix: grey pill
   text `#4f5a68` (the board-light muted value; 5.29:1 on the card2 tint,
   checked) or drop the tint to 10%.
3. **R-25 accent text buttons on card2: 4.40:1.** `.textbtn` uses
   `--m-accent` #1e6fd9; inside an expanded panel (`.xp`, card2) it is 4.40:1
   at 14px: the unit links under Serialized units in the product panel, the
   product, box, and unit links in the location panel, the Ticket and From PO
   links in the unit panel. Tracking already fixed this for `.pkg dd .textbtn`
   with `--m-accent2`. Fix: `.xp .textbtn, .ship .textbtn {color:var(--m-accent2)}`
   (5.85:1).
4. **R-32 focus leaves the dialog.** `openModal` (line 1111) sets
   `aria-modal` but traps nothing. In Adjust, nine Tab presses moved focus to
   the "Stock" nav button behind the backdrop (verified with real key
   presses). Keyboard users end up operating the page under a modal. Fix: on
   Tab/Shift+Tab inside `.modal`, wrap between its first and last focusable
   element; set `inert` on `#app` while `modalStack.length`.
5. **R-32 invisible focus on the file pickers.** `#ntFile` (New ticket, line
   3303) and `#impFile` (Import inventory sheet, line 6663) are 1x1px `.sr`
   inputs that stay in the tab order. Tabbing in New ticket lands on
   `INPUT#ntFile.sr` (measured 1x1px) and nothing on screen shows focus; the
   import screen has two tab stops (the label with `tabindex=0` and the hidden
   input). Fix: `.drop:focus-within{outline:2px solid var(--m-accent);outline-offset:2px}`
   and the same on `#impPick`; give `#impFile` `tabindex="-1"` since the label
   already handles Enter and Space.
6. **R-03 / Playbook "no horizontal scrollbars anywhere": label print
   preview at 360.** `body.preview #printSheet` (line 347) has
   `overflow-x:auto` and 12px side padding around a sheet zoomed to 343px: at
   360 its scrollWidth is 367 against 360, a 7px sideways scroll. Fix: under
   400px use `padding:64px 4px 24px` or `zoom:.4`; drop `overflow-x:auto`.
7. **Task blocked: a short line cannot be ordered from its ticket.** Ticket
   #1042: the HDMI line shows a red "Short 2" chip (line 2905: needs 6, shelves
   hold 4), yet the strip says "To order 0, lines, no PO yet", the key says "To
   order 0", and "Order missing" is disabled with "Every line is covered by
   stock or a PO". `uncovered()` (line 1881) reads the stored `qtyToOrder`,
   which is only recomputed when that line is written, so stock taken by other
   tickets never makes a line orderable. The only way out is Stock, Low stock,
   Reorder, which does not tie the PO to the ticket. Fix: compute uncovered as
   `max(0, needed - staged - available - onOrder)` live, the same sum the Short
   chip uses, and let Order missing and the line's Order button use it.
8. **Data loss risk: two devices can take the same PO number or box label.**
   `nextPoNumber()` (line 1870) is the local max plus one and `draftOps` writes
   the order with a plain `set` (line 1972); `nextBoxLabel()` (line 4981) is
   the first free letter in the local cache and `doReceiveBox` writes the
   container with `set` (line 1474). With the offline-first design (PLAN 9),
   two people starting a draft PO or receiving a box while either is offline
   get the same id: the second `set` replaces the first PO header (vendor,
   ticket, status) while both sets of lines land in one subcollection, or
   replaces the first box's feet and location. By code inspection; not
   reproducible in the demo. Fix: write new orders and boxes in a transaction
   that fails if the doc exists (online), and offline suffix the id with the
   device (7741-a) or hold the number until the server confirms.

### MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate, consistency across views, readability rules)

9. **"To order" is computed four ways.** Tickets list column and the "Has
   items to order" filter count lines with `qtyToOrder > 0`
   (`summarize`, line 1595): #1042 shows an amber "2 lines" and a `.need`
   stripe. The same ticket's strip counts lines `uncovered()` (no PO yet): 0.
   The progress key counts uncovered and not staged (line 2964): 0. The Short
   chip uses live availability (line 2904): 2. #1056 lists "1 line" though its
   only line sits on draft PO 7739. Fix: one helper (finding 7) feeding the
   list column, the filter, the strip, the key, and the board.
10. **"Delivered, not received" has seven phrasings.** Orders pill "Delivered,
    not received"; order line "Delivered, not received"; shipment card
    "Delivered" plus a hint "not received yet"; Tracking pill "Delivered" plus
    "Not received yet, N days since delivery"; Week "Arrived 11/01, not
    received"; ticket chip "delivered 11/01, not received"; board "Arrived
    11/01 on PO 7731, not received yet". Fix: one label, "Delivered, not
    received", everywhere a pill shows it; the date goes beside it.
11. **Pill colors disagree for the same state.** Staged is green on Tickets
    and Week but blue on Items; Staged and Delivered tickets share the same
    green; Open and Closed tickets share grey; Ordered is grey in Orders and
    Tracking, blue in a ticket line chip (`olChipClass`, line 1898), and amber
    "On order" in Week. See the consistency map. Fix: one table of state to
    class used by every view; give Delivered the outlined `.st.done` Week
    already uses.
12. **"Picked" means two things.** Week shows "Picked" (blue) for a line partly
    staged (line 4587); the board shows "Picked" (grey, check) for a line fully
    staged (line 3664); tickets call the partial state "Picking" and the full
    state "Staged". Fix: Week "Partly staged"; board "Staged".
13. **Movement words collide.** Move stock on a product writes type `pick`
    with note "Moved between locations" (line 1487), while moving a unit writes
    `transfer`, labeled "Move". The `return` movement means leftovers from a
    closed ticket going back to a shelf (line 1742), while the unit status
    "Returned" means back from a client and waiting for a look (line 1516).
    Fix: stock moves write `transfer`; rename the unit status label to "Needs a
    look" or the movement to "Back to shelf".
14. **Shipped or In transit.** The same state reads "Shipped" on order line
    pills (`OL_LABEL`, line 885) and "In transit" on shipment cards and
    Tracking (`SHIP_LABEL`, `TR_LABEL`). Fix: "In transit" in `OL_LABEL`.
15. **The same record offers different actions in different places.** A
    product on the Scan card offers Receive, Pick for ticket, Move, Count; the
    same product's Stock panel offers Save, Receive, Adjust, Serialize,
    Reorder, Print label, History (no Move, no Count). A unit on Scan offers
    Stage for ticket; its Items panel does not. Fix: one action list per record
    kind, with Scan showing the subset that fits the phone.
16. **Readability rule 3 misses on items and boxes.** Items written by
    `doPull` (line 1677), `doDeliver` (1707), `doReceiveShipment` (2099),
    Pull everything (3158) carry `ticketId` and `ticketLineId` but no
    `ticketNumber`, and no item ever carries `clientName` (rule 3 names
    clientName on items). Verified in the demo: Deliver to site wrote the unit
    update with holderName and jobName only. Containers carry `pickTicketId`
    with no number (line 2091). The data dictionary (lines 5927-5933) matches
    the build, so it documents the miss. Fix: add `ticketNumber` and
    `clientName` beside every item `ticketId`/`jobId` write and `pickTicketNumber`
    on boxes; add them to `DATA_DICTIONARY`.
17. **Readability rule 1: arrays of objects.** `loadout_shipments.statusLog`
    (grows with every status change, line 1927), `loadout_shipments.lines`
    (line 2003), and ticket line `orderLineRefs` (line 1967) are arrays of
    objects; Export turns each into JSON text in one CSV cell (`flatten`, line
    5816), which is the encoded blob the rule forbids. Fix: statusLog to a
    `shipments/{id}/log` subcollection or a flat `loadout_shipment_events`
    collection; shipment lines are already the order lines, so keep only
    `orderLineIds` (a chip list) plus per-line `qtyShipped`/`qtyReceived` on
    the order line; orderLineRefs is derivable from order lines' ticketLineId.
18. **Unit cost comes from the wrong place on receive.** `doReceiveShipment`
    gives a new serialized unit `cost: r.p.lastCost` (line 2099), not the cost
    fixed on its PO line (`olUnitCost`, line 1933), and the "Gets its own QR"
    decision uses `p.lastCost` too (line 4355). Items out and stock value then
    report a cost the PO never had. Fix: `cost: olUnitCost(r.ol)` and
    `ser` from the same value.
19. **Hover-only "why" text (layoutmobile Hover-Only Interactions, Playbook
    tooltips).** Every non-obvious field explains itself in a `title` on its
    label (dotted underline via `.lab abbr`, line 109). On a phone or tablet no
    `title` ever shows, so the box sheet's "Mark now", "Cut this time", "For
    job", "For ticket", the Receive "Into", and Pull's "Scan or type a code"
    have no reachable explanation on the device they are built for. Fix: make
    `abbr` a button that toggles the same text in a `.hint` under the field,
    or show the hint inline under 640px.
20. **Touch targets between 641 and 899px.** The 44px rule is keyed to
    `max-width:640px` only (line 716). A phone in landscape (667 to 932px) or a
    small tablet gets the desktop top nav (36px buttons), `.btn.sm` at 30px,
    `.linkbtn` rows 20px tall, Week line buttons 30px (measured at 640 where
    the emulated viewport was 640.2px). Fix: also apply the 44px list under
    `(pointer:coarse)`.
21. **R-13 / R-19 amber ring dose and re-pulse.** The decision glow (3px amber
    ring, line 116) is reused as a plain state ring on low consumables
    (`.cons.low`, line 235) and delivered-not-received shipment cards
    (`.ship.dnr`, line 532), so amber ring means "decide here" and "warning".
    And because views redraw with `innerHTML`, every live snapshot restarts the
    three pulses on the Orders list and modal selects. Fix: `.cons.low` and
    `.ship.dnr` use the left stripe `.pkg.dnr` already uses; add `.glow.seen`
    after the first animationend so a redraw does not pulse again.
22. **Playbook tables: some cannot be sorted or arranged.** On Stock,
    Consumables, the "Counted by the each" table is a full `tableHTML` table but
    the Columns button is hidden on that tab (line 3792), so on the phone (thead
    hidden) it cannot be sorted at all. The import dry-run tables (Tabs, Make
    aliases, Products up to 400 rows), the New ticket line preview, and the
    About dictionary are plain tables. Fix: show Columns for "consumables" on
    that tab; build the Products dry-run list with `tableHTML`.
23. **Saved layout does not follow you.** At sign-in the account layout is
    merged under the local one (`Object.assign({}, cloud, local)`, line 7104),
    so a device that has ever saved a layout ignores changes made on another
    device, while the Columns modal says "the same layout follows you to the
    phone". Fix: keep an `updatedAt` on the layout and take the newer one.
