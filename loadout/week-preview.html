Phone frame, 390px wide. Under 640px the nav moves to the bottom tab bar and the week strip stays pinned at the top while the days scroll. Orders and Items sit under More.

Which is which

A. Sections

lists and totals

A week strip over three stacked lists: what is due, what moved, what went out. Reads like a report.

B. Timesheet grid

seven day blocks

The week as a timesheet: each day is a block, each ticket a card inside it, each item a line with its status on the right.

Same in both

  • Install date on tickets. Each ticket sits on the day it installs; the Tickets list sorts by the same date.
  • Previous week / This week / Next week above everything, with the date range.
  • Status pills in the same colors as the ticket screen. Past days are faded so they read as a record; today has a blue border and a Today tag.
  • Unscheduled strip for tickets with no install date, with a date field beside each one.
  • Phone stacking. Under 640px tables turn into cards, day blocks stack with today first, the week strip stays pinned, and every control is at least 44px tall.
