What you are looking at

A. Timecard

Cream paper, stamp-red accent, serif brand, monospace times. Feels like a physical punch card. Warm and readable in daylight, a bit heavy at night.

B. Night Shift

Dark dashboard, glowing green timer, red Punch Out. Best on a phone in a server room. The most "app" of the three and the easiest to keep open all day on a second monitor.

C. Ledger (chosen)

White cards, blue accent, quiet. Matches Backup Audits' light theme, so it sits naturally beside the other Umbrella tools and reads as business software. Dark mode follows the OS.

Same in every direction

  • One running entry at a time. Punch Out and Switch are the only big buttons while clocked in; Client and Job type selects glow amber until chosen when clocked out.
  • Recent chips are the phone fast path: one tap punches in with a known client and job type.
  • Today table is sortable with a Columns manager and stacks into cards under 640px (see the Phone width tab).
  • Client modal never closes on backdrop click. Money field has the dollar sign inside and two decimals.
  • Export preview is exactly the text that gets copied. Toasts sit top-center.
