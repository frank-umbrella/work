Working hours

      
The hours you are normally working. Sundial shows them on the Clock and shades the rest of the day in the Calendar, so anyone looking at the screen can tell whether you are inside your day. Nothing here ever blocks a timer.

Budgets

      
An hours cap for a client, or for one project label inside a client, with optional dates around it. The Clock says where a running entry stands against it, the Export preview lists every budget that touches the range, and you get one warning at 80% and one when it is used up. Nothing is ever blocked: a budget is somebody else's number, agreed in advance, and the useful thing is knowing where you are in it.

      

      

    


    

      
Warnings

      
A job type with a time limit warns you on screen while Sundial is the tab you are looking at. A system notification is what reaches you when it is not.

      
      

    


    

      
Google Calendar

      
Finished entries can be mirrored into a calendar called Sundial in your own Google account, so the week shows up next to your meetings. Sundial only ever asks for the permission that covers calendars it created itself: it cannot see, read or change any of your other calendars, and it never touches an event it did not write.

      
      
      
Already filled in with the Web application client ID for project watchtower-6fbe1, so there is nothing to set up here: press Connect. A browser client ID is a public identifier, not a secret. Replace it only if this app moves to another Google Cloud project; emptying the box comes back to the built-in one. Full steps are in the README.

      

      
      

        
        
        
        
        
      

      
Only finished entries are mirrored. A running timer is added when you stop it, deleting an entry deletes its event, and nothing outside the Sundial calendar is ever read or written.

    


    

      
Drafts

      
While you are typing a new entry - on the Clock or in the manual entry form - Sundial keeps a copy of it on this device, so a closed tab or a flat battery does not take the note with it. Drafts live in this browser only: they are never written to your account and never reach another device. Each one is deleted the moment its entry is started or saved, and by itself after 48 hours.
