# Sundial anti-slop audit 001

Date: 2026-11-06
Mode: DURING (global preference). Audit first, then the safe fixes in the same
release (v0.10.0); anything that would change the design's character is left
for the owner at the bottom.
Scope: the sign-in screen, the Clock, the Timesheet (Day, Week, Calendar),
Clients, Export, Reports, Search, Settings, the footer, toasts and modals, in
both themes, at 360 / 800 / 1280 px. Baseline is v0.9.6 (commit a6a9f8d).

Design Read: a signed-in personal hours tracker for one MSP technician, in a
plain ledger-style language (white cards, one blue accent, Segoe/system sans,
Consolas for numbers), dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1. Direction exists:
design C "Ledger" from `design-preview.html`, as built. The accent is
`--accent` blue; the identity motif is the monospace numbers and the dated
ledger rows. Nothing in this audit asks to restyle that.

Evidence: every ratio below comes from
`python .claude/skills/antislop-human/contrast-check.py "#fg" "#bg"` against
the tokens in the `:root` and dark blocks of `index.html`. Tinted surfaces
(the amber and red chips) were blended by hand first (token at its alpha over
`--card`) and the blended hex was checked. Box sizes are read from the CSS.

Priority follows the rule tier. HIGH = Hard Gate (absolute), MEDIUM =
Purpose-Gate (technique allowed, reason required), LOW = Quality Locks.

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

### 1. Amber text fails AA in the light theme (R-25)

`--glow` (#e0a300) is used as a text colour by `.otflag` (OT), `.pausedline`
("Paused since ..."), `.tagpaused`, `.tagover`, `.pill.paused`,
`.availline.avwarn`, `.sky .k .over`, `.util .over` and
`.limline.lwarn .lchip`. On white it is **2.23:1**, on card2 2.02, inside the
paused pill 2.00: it fails both the normal and the large bar. These are the
warnings, the lines that most need reading. Dark passes (8.27).

Fix: a text-only `--warnink` token (light #8a5d00, dark keeps #e6b13a). Fills,
borders and the demo bar keep `--glow`, so the amber still reads as amber.

### 2. Blue accent as text dips under 4.5:1 on the grey surfaces (R-25)

Light `--accent` #1e6fd9 passes on white (4.85) but not on the page
background (4.48, the footer's Roadmap and Try the demo links), on card2
(4.40, the Week grid's dashed read-only cells `.wgc.ro` and the Settings job
type marker `.mk.on`) or on chip (4.07). The fails are small: under a tenth of
a point.

Fix: darken the light accent one step on the same hue, #1e6fd9 to #1b67cc.
That is 5.03 on bg, 4.93 on card2, 4.57 on chip, and white on the accent
buttons goes from 4.85 to 5.44. `--go` follows it. The dark accent is
untouched.

### 3. Red text on the grey and tinted surfaces (R-25)

`--stop` as text: light 4.17 on card2 (`.mk.off`) and **3.89** in the
hard-limit chip; dark 4.27 on card2 and **3.90** in the hard-limit chip. The
hard-limit chip is the "you are past the limit" line.

Fix: a text-only `--stopink` token (light #b8322d, dark #f08a84). Button
fills and borders keep `--stop`.

### 4. Dark theme Stop buttons fail with white text (R-25)

`.btn.stop` is white on #e0655e = **3.39:1** in dark: the big Stop button,
Erase my data, and every red confirm button in a modal. Light passes (4.60).

Fix: an `--onstop` token, white in light and #0b1420 in dark (5.46), the same
way `--onaccent` already works for the blue buttons.

### 5. Hover icons on the round play and pause buttons drop under 3:1 (R-25, non-text)

`.minibtn.pausebtn:hover` puts a white glyph on amber: 2.23 light, 1.96 dark.
`.minibtn.play:hover` in dark is white on #3ecf8e: 2.00. The icon is the only
thing saying which button you are about to press.

Fix: the hover glyph uses the dark ink (#1a2233 light, #0b1420 dark): 7.12,
9.44 and 9.27.

### 6. Calendar block text on four of the eight client colours (R-25)

White 11px text on green (3.47), amber (2.86), teal (3.18) and rose (4.31).
Imported clients can carry any colour, so this is open-ended.

Fix: each block picks white or #0b1420 ink by whichever has the higher ratio
against its own colour. The colours themselves are unchanged.

### 7. Tap targets under 44px on phones (R-03)

| Element | Size before |
|---|---|
| `.minibtn` play / stop / pause (Today table, End column) | 22 x 22, 4px apart |
| `.ibtn` row actions (Edit, Duplicate, Delete) and Week grid remove | 24 / 22 round |
| `.rtog` Reports breakdown expand | 22 round |
| `.morebtn` Now / Last stop / Clear / All / None / more / less | about 19px tall text |
| `.seg.ampm` AM / PM | about 25 x 28 |
| every other `.seg` button (Day/Week/Calendar, ranges, prev/next) | about 28 tall |
| `.btn.sm` (Columns, the copy buttons, Sign out, Settings actions) | about 29 tall |
| `.themebtn` search and theme | 30 round |
| modal `.x` / sticky toast `.tx` | 30 / 26 round |
| `.swatch` colour picker | 30 round |
| `.chip` (Recent, Reports filters, saved reports) | about 31 tall |
| `.daystrip` day buttons at 360px | about 40 wide x 62 tall |
| Clock skyline day bars at 360px | about 38 wide x 100 tall |
| tab bar buttons | about 58 x 46 (passes) |

Fix: an `@media (pointer: coarse)` block that brings every one of these to a
44px hit area, keeping the small round icons visually small where they sit in
a table (36px visual plus a transparent 4px hit ring, 8px apart). The day
strip and the skyline bars cannot reach 44px wide at 360px without breaking
the seven-column week, which is the point of them: see "Left for the owner".

### 8. No loading or error state on the entry views (R-27)

Before the first entries snapshot arrives, the Clock's Today table says
"Nothing logged today yet.", the Day list "Nothing logged on this day.", the
Week grid "Nothing logged this week", Clients "No clients yet" and Reports
"Nothing matches these filters." That is an empty state shown during a load.
If the snapshot fails (a rules or permission error) the only signal is a
2.8-second "Sync problem" toast, after which the same screens keep saying
nothing was logged. Search is the exception: it already says "Loading older
entries".

Fix: track whether entries and clients have arrived and whether the store
reported an error; until they arrive the empty rows say "Loading your
entries..." and on an error they say what failed and that reloading tries
again.

### 9. The header sits under the iPhone status bar (R-03)

The page declares `viewport-fit=cover` and the tab bar pads
`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, but the sticky `.top` header has no
`env(safe-area-inset-top)`, so opened from the iPhone home screen the brand
and the sync pill sit under the clock and battery. Neither the header nor
`main` pads the left and right insets (landscape notch), and the toast is
fixed at `top:14px`, under the status bar. The sign-in wall does not pad the
insets either.

Fix: the Lights v0.11.1 pattern: `padding-top: calc(10px + env(safe-area-inset-top, 0px))`
on the header, left and right insets on the header, `main`, the footer and the
sign-in wall, and the toast placed below the top inset.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

### 10. Endless blink on the running and syncing dots, with no reduced-motion opt-out (R-19)

`.pill .dot` blinks every 1.6s for as long as a timer runs, and the sync dot
every 1.2s while syncing. Both mark real states, which is why they exist, but
MOTION 1 means hover states only, and an infinite loop is the one motion the
dial forbids outright. Nothing honours `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Fix now (safe): a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` block that stops the
blinks and the Edit details slide. Whether the blink stays at all is an owner
call: see "Left for the owner".

### 11. No way to install from inside the app (C-2, R-26)

The manifest, icons and service worker make Sundial installable, and the
README says the shell opens from the home screen with no signal, but nothing
in the app says how to put it there. On an iPhone there is no prompt at all,
and Safari's Share menu is the only route. This is the v0.10.0 feature work
(Settings card plus the sign-in nudge), listed here so the audit and the
release agree.

## LOW (Quality Locks)

### 12. Spaced hyphens used as dashes in copy (R-02, adjacent)

There is no em dash anywhere in `index.html`, `roadmap.html`, `README.md` or
`PLAN.md` (0 found). Many tooltips and README paragraphs use a spaced hyphen
(" - ") in the em dash's place, for example "Type the hour and minutes - 844
or 8:44 both work". The copywriting skill counts spaced dashes used as
connectors as the same tell. It is also the long-standing house voice
(the product line itself is "Sundial - hours tracker"), so it is an owner
call, not a sweep. New copy in this release uses commas, colons and periods.

### 13. No buzzwords or generic CTAs found (R-15, R-16)

Checked for the R-16 list and the Empty AI Vocabulary list, and for Get
Started / Learn More / Try Now / Explore / Discover. None. "Try the demo"
names what it opens. "Punch" appears only in the roadmap line that records its
removal. Not a defect; recorded so the sweep is on file.

## Left for the owner

- **Day strip and skyline bars at 360px (finding 7).** They are about 40 and
  38px wide on a 360px phone, 44px+ from 390px up (every current iPhone).
  Reaching 44 at 360 means a horizontally scrolling strip or a two-row week,
  both of which change what the strip is. Question: accept the 40px compromise
  on the narrowest Android phones, or redesign the strip for them?
- **Job type flag markers (`.mk`) in Settings.** The dashed / blue / red
  markers inside each job type chip are about 16px tall and three sit
  side by side. Making them 44px means turning them into separate toggles.
  Question: keep them as they are (Settings, rarely touched) or rebuild them as
  a small per-job-type toggle row?
- **The running dot blink (finding 10).** Question: keep the blink (it is
  the "this is counting" cue) with the reduced-motion opt-out added here, or
  hold the dot still, since the timer beside it already counts?
- **Spaced hyphens as dashes (finding 12).** Question: keep as the house
  voice (recorded override), or convert the tooltips and README over time?
- **Sign-in from a home-screen app on iPhone.** Sign-in uses
  `signInWithPopup`. In an iOS home-screen app the Google window can open in
  Safari instead of inside the app the first time, which the new install steps
  say plainly. A redirect flow would avoid it but changes sign-in on every
  device. Question: leave the popup (works, one odd first sign-in) or move to
  redirect sign-in?

## Not findings

- `outline: none` / `outline: 0` appears nowhere in `index.html`. Every
  control keeps the browser focus ring, and `.fld`, `.wgc`, `.brand` and
  `.sky .d` add a visible 2px accent ring on top (R-32).
- Calendar blocks are `role="button" tabindex="0"` and open on Enter and Space;
  the note "more" toggles are real buttons (R-32).
- Modals close only via X, Cancel, the confirm button or Escape, and the
  backdrop click is deliberately inert (house rule, R-26).
- Toasts are top-centre and passive; the sticky warning toast is the one that
  asks a question, and it carries its own buttons and a close (house rule).
- Tables card-stack below 860px, the Week grid swaps to a per-day list, and
  `.tablewrap` loses its sideways scroll at that width (no-horizontal-scroll
  rule, R-03).
- The uppercase card labels (`.card > h4`, 12px, 0.1em tracking) are part of
  the ledger direction, not extreme tracking (R-06, direction).
- Both themes are real: light on `:root`, dark under `prefers-color-scheme`
  and `[data-theme="dark"]`, and the toggle plus the Settings select work
  (R-21, R-34).
- No fabricated numbers: the demo dataset is labelled fictional in a banner,
  in the footer, and on the demo link's tooltip (R-17, R-38).
