Where Sundial is going

Four lists: what is already in the app, what is being built next, what waits for a second person to use it, and what is deliberately never going to happen. The last list is the useful one - a roadmap that only ever says yes is a wish, not a plan.

This page is written by hand and kept beside the app. It is static: no account, no data, nothing to sign in to.

Next

Single-user work, roughly in the order it is worth doing. Everything here fits the app as it stands and needs nothing new underneath it.

RatesBilling rates per job type next A rate lives on the client today. On-site and remote work are often priced differently, so the job type needs to be able to override it and the amounts need to follow.
ReportsClient profitability, lightly Billable hours and amount per client per month, a table and the utilization bar per client. A Reports tab, not a second app.
TimesheetsSubmit and lock a week Mark a week as done and stop it changing underneath you. Useful on your own well before there is anybody to approve it; approval is the team version, further down.
ExportQuickBooks export IIF or CSV in the QuickBooks Time import shape, out of the same rows the hours CSV already builds. The API sync is a team-tier problem.
CalendarScheduling, lightly Planned blocks drawn in grey outline in the Calendar view that a real timer fills in. Answers "what was I supposed to be doing today", which is the other half of "what did I do".
Time offPTO A Time off job type under an internal client, with a daily-hours default, showing in the Week grid and the utilization bar so a week with a holiday in it stops looking like a bad week.

Later

The team tier. All of it needs two things Sundial does not have: a manager role in the security rules, and a capacity per person. None of it is worth building for one person, and all of it is easy once those two exist.

DashboardWho's working Current client, current task, start time, today's total and scheduled end, per person, live. Working hours were built in the shape this will need.
LimitsManager notifications and approvals A time limit that tells somebody other than the person running over it, and an approval instead of a typed reason.
CapacityTeam capacity bar The utilization bar per person and in total.
TimesheetsSubmission and approval Submit a week for approval, with locked weeks once it is approved.
NotificationsBeyond limits A timer left running at the end of the day, a week that has not been submitted.
ChatSlack and Teams Post the daily export to a channel, send limit warnings as a DM.
IntegrationAPI and webhooks A read-only entries feed for the other Umbrella tools.
AccountingXero and QuickBooks sync Once invoicing is real somewhere. Exporting into it comes first.
ForecastingScheduled versus actual over weeks

Not planned

Decided against, with the reason. These are not "someday" - they are the shape of the thing, and changing one of them would make it a different product.

LocationGPS, geofencing, auto clock-in on arrival no A browser cannot track location in the background reliably, and it is more than an hours log should know about a person. The location label per entry covers what anyone actually wanted; mileage per entry covers the expense.
KioskA shared-terminal kiosk mode no For crews clocking in at one screen. This is remote work for an MSP; there is no shared screen.
AppsNative mobile apps no The PWA already does home-screen install, offline and notifications. Native only if push on iOS turns out to be unreliable in practice.
MoneyInvoicing, card payments, expenses with receipts no Invoicing belongs in the accounting system. Sundial exports into it and stops there.
AccountsSSO done Already covered: Google Workspace sign-in restricted to the domain is the single sign-on.
PayOvertime pay maths no Overtime flags a long day and says nothing about what it is worth. Payroll rules are a payroll system's job and getting them subtly wrong is worse than not having them.
