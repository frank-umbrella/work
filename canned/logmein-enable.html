Re-enable LogMeIn — step by step

When a computer shows “Not accessible — LogMeIn is off,” the LogMeIn service on that PC has been switched off and no one can connect to it remotely. These steps turn it back on from the LogMeIn Control Panel.

Windows 10 & Windows 11

Send this to the client, or walk them through it. The person sitting at the computer has to do this — because LogMeIn is off, we can't do it remotely. When they're done, the PC should read “Accessible” and we can connect.

1

Open the LogMeIn Control Panel from Start

Click the Start button (or press the Windows key), type LogMeIn Control Panel, and select it from the search results.

If LogMeIn Control Panel doesn't appear in the results, restart the computer and try again.

2

Click the power button to turn it on

In the upper-left corner of the Control Panel, find the LogMeIn power button. If it shows “Not accessible” (a red power button), click Turn on to switch it back on.

This is the screen you'll see when LogMeIn is off. Clicking the red power button or Turn on both re-enable it.

3

Confirm it now shows “Accessible”

Once it's on, the power button turns green and the status reads “Accessible” or “Enabled and Online.” That's it — we can connect now.

Not in the search results? If “LogMeIn Control Panel” doesn't appear when you search from Start, restart the computer and try again — or use the system-tray method below.

Alternative — open it from the system tray

★

Find the LogMeIn icon in the system tray

Look in the notification area, near the clock in the bottom-right corner of the screen. If you don't see the blue LogMeIn icon, click the “Show hidden icons” up-arrow to reveal it.

★

Right-click it, open the Control Panel, click the power button

Right-click the LogMeIn icon, click “LogMeIn Control Panel,” then click the power button in the upper-left corner to re-enable LogMeIn (Step 2 above).

Last thing: please leave the computer powered on and connected to the internet after these steps, so we can reach it.
