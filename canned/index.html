Gmail

Get the full header of an email

Send when you need a client to pull the full message header from an email in Gmail (for spoofing / phishing / delivery investigations).

How to view and copy the full header of an email in Gmail:

1. From a browser, open Gmail and open the email you want to check.
2. Next to "Reply," click "More" (the three vertical dots in the upper-right corner of the email), then click "Show original."
3. The full header opens in a new window.
4. Click "Copy to clipboard" and send it back to us.

Sources: Google – Trace an email with its full header · Lifewire – See full headers in Gmail

Windows & Mac

Locate your PC name

Send the block that matches the client's platform. The Run-box option works on both Windows 10 and Windows 11 if you're not sure which they have.

Windows 11

To find your PC name on Windows 11:

1. Press the Windows key and "I" at the same time to open Settings.
2. Select "System" at the top left of the screen that opens.
3. Your computer's name is shown at the top of the pane on the right. You can also scroll down to "About" and click it — your name is listed next to "Device name."

Windows 10

To find your PC name on Windows 10:

1. Right-click the Start button.
2. Click "System."
3. Under "Computer name, domain, and workgroup settings," you'll find your computer name (and its full computer name if the PC is on a domain).

Any Windows (Run box) — Windows 10 or 11

To find your PC name on Windows 10 or 11:

1. Press the Windows key and "R" at the same time to open the "Run" box.
2. In the "Run" box, type:  cmd /k hostname
3. Press Enter. A command prompt window opens with your computer name at the top.

Mac (macOS 10.4 or later)

To find your computer name on a Mac:

1. Open System Preferences (or System Settings).
2. Click the "Sharing" pane.
3. Your computer name is listed at the top of the pane.

Microsoft 365

Open a delegated mailbox (Outlook on the web)

Send when a client has been granted access to another mailbox and needs to open it in Outlook on the Web (OWA).

To open a mailbox you've been given access to, using Outlook on the web:

1. Sign in to https://outlook.office.com with your own email.
2. In the top-right corner, click your profile picture / initials.
3. Select "Open another mailbox."
4. Type the mailbox owner's name or email, select it, then click "Open."
5. The other user's mailbox opens in a new browser tab.
