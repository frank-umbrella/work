Spool design preview v0.1

Spool turns an approved Portal proposal into a pick ticket, tracks every product line from vendor order to staging bay to job site, and keeps the shelf count honest, including feet left on each box of wire. Three visual directions below, four screens each. The markup is identical across directions; only the theme changes. Read PLAN.md next to this file for the build plan; the direction pick is decision D9 there.

Reading this as: a staff warehouse app for two to five people on phones and one desktop, in the Backup Audits family, dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1. Every client, person, address, order number, and count in the mock is invented sample data. Only the direction buttons, the view tabs, and the mock's own nav links do anything; every other button is a picture.

Direction Left / right arrow keys switch directions. Tabs below switch screens. Narrow the window under 640px to see the card-stacked tables and the bottom tab bar.

Three directions

A. Rack

dark, scanner-first

Near-black with amber kept for the one tile or control that needs a decision, and for the scan button. All quantities in a monospace face so columns line up at a glance. Buttons are 44px tall so gloved thumbs hit them. Best in a dim rack room; heavy on a bright office monitor.

B. Ledger ★

light, matches Backup Auditsrecommended

White cards, blue accent, the same family as Backup Audits and Sundial's chosen theme, so it sits next to the other staff tools and reads as business software. Its own mark: area headers are a ruled line, and anything printed on a box or a shipping label (container names, tracking numbers) is set in the mono face.

C. Manifest

warm, paper pick ticket

Cream paper, ruled lines, serif headings, and statuses stamped like a rubber stamp. Feels like the clipboard that hung on the bay. Friendly in print and in daylight; the stamps take more room in narrow tables.

What you are looking at

  • Ticket is pick ticket #1042 for Acme Dental, mid-pick: lines grouped by Portal area, with order and tracking chips on each line and one amber line that still has no PO. The Portal note on the right is exactly what Copy for Portal puts on the clipboard.
  • Stock is the catalog with low stock on top, then consumables sold by the foot with each box's last reading. Cat5e is under par and offers a two-box reorder.
  • Orders lists open POs; the draft at the top has no vendor yet, so its select glows amber. PO 7731 shows per-line status, the ticket line each serves, and two shipments, one received into Bay 2.
  • Scan is the phone: camera with a scan box, typed fallback, and the feet-left sheet for Cat6 blue box 3 with quick subtract buttons.
  • Same in every direction: tables stack into cards under 640px, every control grows to 44px there, the sync pill is the only place offline shows, toasts sit top-center under the top bar, and tooltips explain the non-obvious fields.
  • Not drawn, specified in PLAN.md section 4: the empty, loading, offline, and error state of each screen, with its exact wording.
