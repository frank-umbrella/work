Date: 2026-10-23. Mode: AFTER (session override). Scope: `work/spool/design-preview.html`
(three directions, four views) and `work/spool/PLAN.md`. Reviewer: Fable 5.1.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM,
Quality Locks = LOW. Nothing is changed until you pick numbers.

Contrast ratios below come from the skill's `contrast-check.py`; mixed colors
(`color-mix`) were resolved by hand in sRGB before checking.

## Findings

### HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **R-25 Ledger status text fails AA.** `.st.warn` text (#a36e24 after mix)
   on its tint is 3.75:1; `.st.ok` is 4.00:1; `.chip.warn` on card2 is 3.95:1;
   `.mv.read` on white is 4.14:1. All are 11-12px text. Fix: in the Ledger
   theme, mix more text into the status colors (warn 55%, ok 60%) or set
   darker text-only variables (`--m-warn-text:#8a5a14`, `--m-ok-text:#166b3e`).
2. **R-25 Manifest muted text fails AA.** `--m-muted:#7a7260` is 4.50:1 on the
   card (fails unrounded), 4.12:1 on card2, 3.94:1 on the page background. That
   color carries every label, hint, date, and location. Fix: `--m-muted:#655d4b`
   (about 5.6:1 on the card).
3. **R-25 Muted zeros are near-invisible.** `.z{opacity:.6}` on muted text puts
   Ledger's zeros at 2.54:1 (worse in Manifest). Fix: drop the opacity and keep
   the muted color, or render zero as a dash.
4. **R-25 Ledger tracking link on card2.** `.trk` uses the accent (#1e6fd9) on
   #f1f4f8 at 13px: 4.40:1. Fix: use `--m-accent2` (#155cb8) for link text on
   card2 surfaces, or underline and darken.
5. **R-03 Tap targets under 44px on the phone.** Ledger sets `--m-tap:38px`
   and `--m-tapsm:30px`; Manifest 40/32. Row actions (`.btn.sm`) in the
   card-stacked tables are 30-32px tall at phone width, where thumbs use them.
   Fix: inside the `@container (max-width:640px)` block, `.btn{min-height:44px}`
   and `.btn.sm{min-height:44px}`; keep the smaller desktop sizes.
6. **R-38 / R-18 Fabricated people and places shown as real.** Timeline names
   "Dana" and "Luis", clients "Acme Dental", "Harbor Vet", "Northside Legal",
   the address "1180 Larkin Ave, Suite 200", and vendor order "SO 4418873" are
   invented and nothing on the page says so. Fix: keep the sample data (a mock
   needs rows) but say it once where it is seen: a "Sample data" tag in the
   mock footer and one sentence in the intro, and replace the two invented
   staff names with roles ("Warehouse", "Tech").
7. **R-27 No empty, loading, or error state anywhere.** The mock shows the
   happy path only, and PLAN.md section 4 never says what an empty Tickets
   list, an empty catalog, an offline write, or a failed CSV parse looks like.
   Fix (plan, not mock): add a "States" paragraph to each screen in section 4
   with the exact copy, e.g. "No pick tickets yet. Import a Portal CSV to start
   one." and "Could not read column headers. Pick them below."
8. **R-37 Design Read and dials never declared.** Direction exists implicitly
   (match Backup Audits, phone-first warehouse), but no one wrote it down, so
   the three directions cannot be judged against it. Fix: add one line to
   PLAN.md D9 and the preview intro: "Reading this as: a staff warehouse app
   for two to five people on phones and one desktop, in the Backup Audits
   family, dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1."

### MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

9. **R-01 / Part 1 "Excessive accent" in Rack contradicts its own caption.**
   The caption says "amber where you have to decide", yet all five stat tiles,
   the nav, the laser, the scan button, and the search chips are amber. Fix:
   Rack counts in `--m-text`; amber only on the To-order tile, the primary
   button, and the active scan tab.
10. **R-07 Grid pattern inside the fake camera.** `.cam:before` draws a 22px
    grid over the viewfinder for no stated reason; real camera previews have no
    grid. Fix: remove the grid overlay, keep the scan box and the hint.
11. **R-13 / R-19 Glow count in the Scan view.** Scan box glow, laser glow, the
    active scan tab's accent shadow, plus the amber attention glow elsewhere:
    four glows. Fix: drop the laser glow and the box glow; keep the attention
    glow and the scan tab.
12. **R-19 Endless pulse on the attention glow.** `.fld.glow` animates forever.
    This is your own Playbook rule (amber glow on unset decision dropdowns), so
    under R-37 the question is yours: keep the infinite pulse, or keep the glow
    static and pulse only three times on page load. Recommendation: static
    glow, three pulses.
13. **R-06 Uppercase wide-tracked labels everywhere.** `.mcard h4`, `.sv .k`,
    `.meta dt`, `.tbl th`, `.mv`, `.rk`, `.ship dt`, and the switcher label all
    use uppercase with .07-.1em tracking. The reason is defensible (labels must
    read as labels next to dense numbers), but it is not written down, and
    Manifest already shows the alternative. Fix: write the one-line reason in
    PLAN.md section 11, and drop the tracking to .04em so it reads as a label,
    not a hero eyebrow.
14. **R-20 Ledger has the weakest identity, and it is the recommended one.**
    Swap the spool mark and Ledger is any light admin template. Its family
    resemblance to Backup Audits is the stated reason, which is legitimate, but
    it needs one motif of its own. Fix: give Ledger the area-group headers a
    1.5px rule in the text color (the Manifest treatment, without the serif),
    and keep tracking numbers and container labels in the mono face. Both are
    tied to what the app does.
15. **R-01 Left stripes: one carries state, one is decoration.** `.need` rows
    get a 3px warn stripe (real state: needs ordering) and Manifest `.paper`
    gets a 4px tan stripe plus ruled lines (clipboard motif). Fix: write both
    reasons in PLAN.md section 11; no code change.
16. **R-11 Playbook checklist is missing the human rules.** PLAN.md section 11
    has no line for contrast, keyboard reachability, focus rings, or the three
    UI states, so the build would repeat findings 1-7. Fix: add four lines.

### LOW (Quality Locks)

17. **R-24 / R-26 Dead controls in the mock are not labeled as such.** "More"
    in the nav and tab bar, "Expand", "Show all 31", and every row button do
    nothing. A mock is allowed that, but the intro should say it once: "Only
    the direction buttons, view tabs, and nav links work; the rest is a
    picture." Also give "More" a destination in the plan (it is Settings,
    Clients, Movements, Labels).
18. **R-36 Aphorism formula.** "Readings are the truth; picks are estimates."
    (Stock view and PLAN.md section 7). Fix: "A reading replaces the estimate
    the picks built up."
19. **Toast covers the breadcrumb.** The permanent demo toast sits over
    "Tickets / Picking" on phone width. Fix: move it into the intro as a
    sentence, or position it under the top bar at 12px.
20. **R-29 Palette count per theme.** Accent + ok + warn + danger + info is
    five hues before the two carrier brand colors. Ledger folds info into the
    accent (four). Status colors are a system, so this passes, but write it
    down in section 11 as the system (one accent, three status hues, carrier
    marks only on shipment chips).

## Liveliness block (for the record)

- Dials proposed: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1 for all three directions
  (staff tool, data-dense, used while holding a box). Finding 8 makes this
  explicit.
- Focal point per screen: Ticket = the lines table; Stock = the low-stock
  container; Orders = the draft PO's glowing vendor select; Scan = the feet
  number. Pass.
- One deliberate accent: Ledger and Manifest pass; Rack fails (finding 9).
- Identity motif: Rack = amber mono counts; Manifest = stamps and ruled paper;
  Ledger = none yet (finding 14).

- Mobile 375px and pane width, all 12 theme-view combinations: no overflow.
- Everything inside the mock other than the above is a picture (finding 17).
